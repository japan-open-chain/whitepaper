# 8. Organization and Governance

## 8.1 Validators and Their Role

Japan Open Chain's Validators are selected from major Japanese companies, starting with Dentsu, Hatena, and pixiv. They are responsible for the stability of the network and the correctness of transactions, and as organizations deeply rooted in Japan's business ecosystem, they strengthen the trustworthiness of Japan Open Chain. To promote diversity and sustained growth of the ecosystem, trusted financial groups, media companies, and other crypto-related companies and startups are also expected to join as Validators.

Under PoSA, each Validator stakes 32 JOC and performs the duties of attestation, block proposal, and sync-committee participation ([4.6.3](04-japan-open-chain.md#463-validator-duties-rewards-and-penalties)). Validator entry is controlled by the consortium ([4.7](04-japan-open-chain.md#47-authority-layer)), and malicious signatures are slashed by the consensus layer.

## 8.2 Japan Open Chain's Operating Organization and Its Responsibilities

Japan Blockchain Foundation Inc. is the operator of the Japan Open Chain consortium and sits at its center. It sets up and runs the Japan Open Chain secretariat and works for the stable operation of the community. Under PoSA, it operates the Safe Multisig that owns the Authority layer's `ConsortiumManager`, and is responsible for registering Validators, executing deposits, and forced exits ([4.7](04-japan-open-chain.md#47-authority-layer)).

Japan Blockchain Foundation Inc. conducted the IEO after review by a crypto-asset exchange supervised by JVCEA (Japan Virtual and Crypto assets Exchange Association), a certified payment services association, and by the Financial Services Agency. Since the IEO, it has been continuously monitored by that exchange on its disclosure of project progress, its proper management of the funds raised, and similar matters.

For details, see the official website: https://www.jbfd.org/

## 8.3 Japan Open Chain Partners

Japan Open Chain values collaboration with a wide range of stakeholders. In particular, it promotes partnerships with many organizations through its Development Partner program. All users and partners in this ecosystem are essential to Japan Open Chain's growth and innovation, and together we aim to create new value. Partner details are updated regularly on the official website (https://www.japanopenchain.org).

# 2. Market Background and Opportunity

## 2.1 Growth of the Blockchain Market

Blockchain technology continues to evolve, and markets built on it are growing rapidly. They are expected to keep growing, with the potential to raise global GDP by USD 1.76 trillion and Japan's GDP by USD 72 billion by 2030.

The benefits of blockchain are recognized and being adopted in real business, especially in fintech, healthcare, logistics, manufacturing, and energy. Reported use cases include more efficient cross-border transactions in finance, management and sharing of medical data, and supply-chain transparency.

## 2.2 Growth of the NFT Market

Since around 2021, NFTs (non-fungible tokens) and DeFi (decentralized finance) have attracted growing attention. Monthly NFT trading volume rose by 5,438% between January and December 2021. Annual trading volume across the top 10 NFT marketplaces in 2021 reached USD 239 billion, more than 65% of it on Ethereum mainnet.

Speculative trading has since cooled, and annual NFT trading volume in 2024 was about USD 13.7 billion (DappRadar). NFTs first drew attention in art and gaming, but new use cases that exploit their properties are increasing — certificates such as membership cards and IDs, tickets, and more — and further growth is expected.

## 2.3 The Arrival of DeFi

With DeFi, NFTs and financial products can be traded using programs on the blockchain, so financial transactions can be programmed without an intermediary.

TVL (total value locked), which measures the funds supplied to the market, rose by about 1,070% from USD 15.8 billion in January 2021 to USD 169.2 billion in December 2021. Through the market's ups and downs since then, about USD 96 billion was still deposited in DeFi as of September 2026, with Ethereum accounting for more than 50% (DefiLlama).

DeFi is expected to absorb the whole financial industry, including securities and real estate, and to create an entirely new financial world. Because Japan Open Chain is operated by Japanese companies with high legal stability, it is a very accessible blockchain for DeFi as well.

## 2.4 Growth of the Stablecoin Market

Since 2020, the stablecoin market has also grown dramatically.

A stablecoin is a crypto-asset whose value is pegged to a specific asset, currency, or basket of assets. Stablecoins were introduced to mitigate the risk of large crypto-asset price swings, and their presence in the market has grown in recent years.

With the arrival of DeFi in particular, stablecoins serve as a medium of exchange and as collateral, and many DeFi protocols use them. Many stablecoins — Tether (USDT), USDC, DAI, and others — are in circulation, their total market value is rising rapidly, and total issuance reached about USD 280 billion as of November 2026 (DefiLlama).

The main advantage of stablecoins is price stability, but they also offer cheaper transfer fees than existing financial infrastructure and the properties of programmable money. They are expected to enable many applications, such as more efficient cross-border transfers and trades and wider access to financial services. In developing countries in particular, people without access to traditional banking can receive financial services through stablecoins, even without a bank account.

On the other hand, some so-called stablecoins are issued without legal asset backing, are not actually stable in value, and some are close to fraud. Following the revision of Japanese law on stablecoins, Japan Open Chain is already running proof-of-concept trials, together with Validators and financial institutions, to issue legally robust stablecoins whose backing assets are held by banks.

Thanks to its legal stability and transaction speed, Japan Open Chain is expected to play a major role in stablecoin issuance and circulation.
